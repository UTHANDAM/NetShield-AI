# NetShield AI — SOC Analyst & Operator User Manual

Welcome to **NetShield AI**, an intelligent Network Anomaly Detection and Threat Monitoring SOC platform. This manual walks security analysts, incident responders, and administrators through day-to-day operations.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Logging In

1. Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Select your persona or enter credentials:
   - **Administrator**: `admin@netshield.ai` | `Admin@123456`
   - **SOC Analyst**: `analyst@netshield.ai` | `Analyst@123456`
   - **Security Viewer**: `viewer@netshield.ai` | `Viewer@123456`
3. Upon successful login, you will land on the **Live SOC Command Center**.

---

## 2. Live SOC Command Center (Dashboard)

The central command center provides real-time situational awareness:
- **KPI Metrics**: Active Incidents, 24h Alerts, Mean Time to Respond (MTTR), and Average Threat Confidence.
- **Real-Time Attack Simulation**:
  - Click **Simulate DDoS**, **SYN Port Scan**, **SSH Password Spray**, or **SQL Injection** at the top right of the dashboard to inject live attack traffic and observe automated detection.
- **Protocol Distribution**: Visual breakdown of TCP, UDP, ICMP, and HTTP traffic volume.
- **Live Anomaly Feed**: Streaming network flows with severity badges (`CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`).

---

## 3. Network Traffic Telemetry

Navigate to **Network Traffic** (`/network-traffic`):
- Displays deep packet inspection (DPI) flow logs including Source IP, Destination IP, Ports, Protocols, Packet Length, and ML Anomaly Scores.
- Toggle between **All Flows** and **Anomalies Only**.
- Filter by specific IP addresses, subnets, or threat classifications.

---

## 4. Threat Alerts & Triage

Navigate to **Threat Alerts** (`/threat-alerts`):
- Review real-time alerts generated by both ML models and SIEM detection rules.
- Click any alert to view its granular payload details, confidence score, and feature vector.
- **Disposition Actions**:
  - Mark as **Investigating** to assign to your queue.
  - Mark as **Resolved** upon completion.
  - Mark as **False Positive** to tune future detection thresholds.

---

## 5. Correlated Incidents & 1-Click SOAR Playbooks

Navigate to **Incidents** (`/incidents`):
- Security Incidents aggregate related alerts affecting the same target or originating from coordinated attack infrastructure.

### Investigating an Incident:
1. Click on an Incident from the list to enter the **Incident Detail & Remediation Console**.
2. **Timeline Notes**: Add investigation observations, forensic artifacts, or shift handover notes.
3. **Execute 1-Click SOAR Playbooks**:
   - 🛡️ **Block Threat IP**: Instantly deploys firewall blocking rules and updates IOC blacklist.
   - 🔒 **Isolate Compromised Host**: Quarantines the affected internal asset onto an isolated VLAN.
   - ⚡ **Enforce Rate Limiting**: Applies QoS traffic throttling to stop volumetric bandwidth exhaustion.
   - 🔑 **Revoke User Session**: Invalidates active JWT tokens to contain identity compromise.
   - 📄 **Run Automated Forensics**: Triggers a memory dump and generates a comprehensive forensic report.

---

## 6. Threat Intelligence & MITRE ATT&CK Matrix

Navigate to **Threat Intelligence** (`/threat-intelligence`):
- **MITRE ATT&CK Matrix**: Visualizes active enterprise tactics (Reconnaissance, Initial Access, Execution, Persistence, Command & Control, Exfiltration, Impact) with real-time incident heatmaps.
- **IOC Database**: Search and review malicious IPs, domains, SHA-256 hashes, and CVE identifiers with threat reputation scores.
- **SIEM Detection Rules**:
  - View existing heuristic and signature rules.
  - Create new detection rules with custom conditions and MITRE tactic tags.

---

## 7. AI Models & Sandbox

Navigate to **AI Models** (`/ai-models`):
- Inspect model accuracy, ROC curves, confusion matrices, and feature importance charts for CIC-IDS-2017 and UNSW-NB15 pipelines.
- **Inference Sandbox**: Manually input network flow parameters to test real-time classification and response latency.

---

## 8. Forensic Reports & SIEM Export

Navigate to **Reports** (`/reports`):
- **Generate Executive & Technical PDF Report**: Click **Download PDF Report** to create a publication-ready SOC executive brief with audit logs, charts, and remediation histories.
- **CSV Data Export**: Export structured traffic and alert data for external SIEM (Splunk, Elastic, Sentinel) ingestion.
